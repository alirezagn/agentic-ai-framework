# DECISIONS — my-project

Record every meaningful choice as a `### DEC-NNN:` section. While a decision has
status `PROPOSED_CHANGE`, the orchestrator blocks the affected tasks until a
human approves or rejects it (`orchestrator.approve_decision`).
